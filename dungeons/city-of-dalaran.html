City of Dalaran

New in ForeverLevels 28–33 · run at 29–33Alterac Mountains (sewer entrance)

The barrier around Dalaran has fallen and the city is full of misfiring magic. Horde players start the attunement in Tarren Mill and enter through the sewers.
