Ruins of Lordaeron

New in ForeverLevels 15–20 · run at 15–17Above the Undercity, Tirisfal Glades

The Forsaken are reclaiming the Scourge-haunted ruins above the Undercity. Every Horde quest starts in Tirisfal, the Undercity or Silverpine, so pick them all up before you go in.
