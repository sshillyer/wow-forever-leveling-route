Alliance Leveling Route

Night Elf start, Teldrassil to 60. Darkshore and Ashenvale take you to 20, then the boat from Auberdine to Menethil Harbor brings you into the main Alliance route through the Wetlands, Duskwood and beyond. Forever's new zones and dungeons are slotted in where their Alliance quests are on your path.

Questing zone Dungeon slot New New in Forever

How to use this

  • Keep your guide addon running for the step-by-step. This page is the overview: which zone you should be in, and which dungeon to fit in around it.
  • Darnassus, Auberdine and Astranaar give the Blackfathom Deeps quests. Pick them up before you leave Kalimdor; you won't want to come back for them.
  • The Auberdine boat goes to Menethil Harbor. From there, the Ironforge tram reaches Stormwind for the Deadmines, the Stockade and the Dalaran quests.
  • Level ranges for the new Forever dungeons differ a little between sources. Windows here use the middle of what sources agree on.
  • Quest data for the early dungeons comes from the beta build. Check it on the launch client.
