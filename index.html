Horde Leveling Route

Undead start, Tirisfal to 60. The questing spine is the classic Horde route that TUGs and most guides follow, with Forever's new zones and dungeons slotted in. Dungeon slots are placed where their Horde quests are on your path and their rewards still matter.

How to use this

  • Keep TUGs running for the step-by-step. This page is the overview: which zone you should be in, and which dungeon to fit in around it.
  • Pick up a dungeon's quests on your way through its hub, then run it once you're in the "go" window. Most early quest rewards are blues or strong greens that carry you for 8 to 10 levels.
  • Your dualbox pairs can't clear these alone. Plan one group run per dungeon with all its quests in hand.
  • Level ranges for the new Forever dungeons differ a little between sources (Excavation Site is listed as 24 to 29 and 26 to 31, for example). Windows here use the middle of what sources agree on.
  • Quest data for the early dungeons comes from the October 8 beta build. Check it on the launch client.
